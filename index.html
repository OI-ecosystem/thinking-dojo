The Thinking Dojo

A structured system that teaches children how to think.

Not what to think — but how to process, decide, and act.  ·  Ages 7–13

What It Is

Structured thinking for children.

Most systems teach children what to think. Very few teach them how to think.

The Thinking Dojo is a calm, structured progression system. Children move through seven belt levels — each one developing a specific thinking skill.

No scores. No tests. No pressure. Just clear, structured thinking — built step by step.

Notice  →  Pattern  →  Choice  →  Plan  →  Act  →  Review  →  Independent Thinking
The Seven Levels

Colour is Clarity.

Each belt develops one specific thinking skill. Children progress naturally at their own pace.

White
Notice — see what is happening clearly
Yellow
Pattern — recognise what repeats
Orange
Choice — understand your options
Green
Plan — think ahead clearly
Blue
Act — turn thinking into action
Brown
Review — learn from outcomes
Black
Independent Thinker — think without being told
What Children Develop

Real skills. Real situations.

The Thinking Dojo develops thinking through everyday situations — not worksheets or tests.

  • Notice what matters in any situation
  • Recognise patterns in what happens around them
  • Think through options before deciding
  • Plan with clear steps
  • Act with intention and follow through
  • Review what happened and learn from it
  • Think independently — without being guided

The System
