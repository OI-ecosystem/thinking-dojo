How It Works

A process, not a test.

The Thinking Dojo is designed to be used in real moments — not in a classroom, not as homework. A card pulled at dinner. A question asked after a difficult situation. A session on the Coach Engine before bed. The thinking happens in life, not about life.

1

Identify where your child is now

Use the Thinking Progress Chart to observe — not test — your child's natural thinking. Which belt level best describes how they currently approach situations? There is no wrong answer.

2

Use the cards in everyday moments

Pull one card from the relevant belt pack. Ask the question naturally — at the table, in the car, after something happened. One question. Let them think. Don't correct. Don't guide. Just listen.

3

Use the Coach Engine for guided sessions

When your child is ready for a structured session, open the Coach Engine. Six guided questions per belt level. The screen changes colour as they progress. Private and licensed — their responses belong to them.

4

Progress naturally — no pressure

When you notice your child thinking at the next level naturally and consistently, move them up. Progress is observational. There is no test, no pass mark, no timeline. Every child moves at their own pace.

The System

Three formats. One framework.

The Thinking Dojo is available as physical materials, a story series, and a digital coaching engine — all built around the same seven-level framework. Each format works independently. Together they create a complete thinking environment.

Thinking Cards

Printable card sets by belt level. Seven packs — one per belt. Each card carries a single question designed to activate the specific thinking skill for that level. Print, cut, and use in any real moment. No setup. No screen.

Story Series

Seven short stories — one per belt level. Each story follows Kai and Tana through a real-world situation that requires the thinking skill of that belt. Children read the situation, see the thinking in action, and recognise the skill in their own life.

The Coach Engine

A digital coaching system that guides a child through their current belt level with six structured questions per session. The screen changes colour to match the active belt. Sessions are private and licensed — responses belong to the child, not a platform. Works on iPhone, iPad, and desktop without an app download.

  • Colour-progressive interface — each belt has its own visual environment
  • Six guided questions per level — structured but never prescriptive
  • Licensed access — one purchase, permanent use, all devices
