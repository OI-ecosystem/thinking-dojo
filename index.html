How it works

A process, not a test.

The thinking happens in real moments, not in a classroom or as homework. Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Find their belt

    Use the Thinking Progress Chart to observe how your child thinks right now. There is no wrong answer.

  2. 2

    Use the cards

    Pull one card from their belt pack in an everyday moment. Ask it, then let them think. Don't correct, just listen.

  3. 3

    Add a guided session

    When they're ready, open the Coach for six calm questions at their level. The screen takes on their belt colour.

  4. 4

    Move up naturally

    When they think at the next level on their own, move them up. No pass mark and no timeline.

The Thinking Progress Chart

Every pack includes the chart. Stick it on the fridge. It describes each belt in one plain sentence, so you can see where your child is and what comes next without testing them.

Questions parents ask

Before you choose.

What age is it for?

The Thinking Dojo is designed for children aged 7–13. Younger children usually spend longer on the early belts, which is exactly how it should work.

Which pack should I start with?

If you want to try the idea, start with the Workbook. For everyday use at home, the Starter System gives you the stories, cards and chart. The Complete System adds a Parent Guide and premium cards. Add the Coach Engine if your child would enjoy guided sessions on a screen.

Does my child need a screen?

No. The workbook, stories, cards and chart are all used on paper in everyday moments. Only the Coach Engine is on a screen, and it is calm by design with no ads or points.

Where does my child start?

Most children start at White (Notice). Use the Thinking Progress Chart to observe how your child thinks now and pick the belt that fits best. There is no wrong answer.

What devices does the Coach Engine work on?

It runs in the web browser on iPhone, iPad and desktop. There is no app to download.
