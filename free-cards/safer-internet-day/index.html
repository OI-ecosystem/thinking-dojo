Free cards/Safer Internet Day

Three thinking cards for Safer Internet Day.

Notice, choose, review. One question for each, to help children reflect on their own screen time around Tuesday 9 February 2027.

White Belt · Notice
What did you notice today?
The Thinking Dojo
Orange Belt · Choice
What might happen if you choose this?
The Thinking Dojo
Brown Belt · Review
What would you do differently next time?
The Thinking Dojo

How to use them

  1. First: noticeAsk the White Belt card, then steer towards screens. How did you feel before you picked it up, and after you put it down?
  2. Next: chooseAsk the Orange Belt card about one more episode, stopping now and setting a timer.
  3. Then: reviewAsk the Brown Belt card about one evening this week. Go first with an answer about your own phone.

The full routine is in Three questions for Safer Internet Day.

Safer Internet Day is coordinated in the UK by the UK Safer Internet Centre, which publishes its own free resources at saferinternet.org.uk. The Thinking Dojo is not part of the campaign.
