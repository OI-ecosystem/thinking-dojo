About the Thinking Dojo

What is the Thinking Dojo?

A thinking system for children aged 7–13. There are seven coloured belts, and each one is a real thinking skill. Children move from Notice to Independent Thinker through cards, stories and short guided sessions, at their own pace.

What are the seven belts?

Notice (White). Sees what is happening and can describe it clearly. Pattern (Yellow). Begins to recognise repetition and simple connections. Choice (Orange). Makes simple decisions and starts thinking about what might happen next. Plan (Green). Thinks ahead and begins to organise actions step by step. Act (Blue). Acts with intention and adjusts while doing. Review (Brown). Understands what happened and begins learning from outcomes. Independent Thinker (Black). Thinks clearly, independently, and with structure.

Is it a course or a test?

Neither. There are no scores, no tests, no pass mark and no timeline. Progress is observed, never marked.

What age is it for?

The Thinking Dojo is designed for children aged 7–13. Younger children usually spend longer on the early belts, which is exactly how it should work.

Using it at home

Where does my child start?

Most children start at White (Notice). Use the Thinking Progress Chart to observe how your child thinks now and pick the belt that fits best. There is no wrong answer.

How much time does it take?

A card takes one moment: you pull one at dinner or in the car, ask the question, and listen. A Coach Engine session is a guided 10–15 minutes.

What if my child just says “I don’t know”?

Go back to noticing. Ask what they saw or heard, and wait. If nothing comes, tell them one thing you noticed today. Don’t correct the answer when it does come. There is more in what to ask instead of “How was school?”

How can I practise noticing at home?

Try the kitchen game. One evening, move something in the kitchen, such as the toaster. The next morning, ask your child what is different about the kitchen today.

Noticing what has changed is the first step towards spotting patterns. It also helps children stay aware of what is around them.

How do I know when to move up a belt?

When your child thinks at the next level on their own, move them up. There is no pass mark and no timeline.

Does my child need a screen?

No. The workbook, stories, cards and chart are all used on paper in everyday moments. Only the Coach Engine is on a screen, and it is calm by design with no ads or points.

Teachers and home educators

Can I use it with a whole class?

Yes. You can ask a whole class one question while you read a story, such as “What did you notice?”, and each pupil can write an answer on a printed card. A card also works as a five-minute starter with one question on the board. See the Thinking Dojo in the classroom and a week of starters.

Choosing and buying

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. Print seven free cards, one from every belt, and try one at dinner tonight.

Which pack should I start with?

If you want to try the idea, start with the Workbook. For everyday use at home, the Starter System gives you the stories, cards and chart. The Complete System adds a Parent Guide and premium cards. Add the Coach Engine if your child would enjoy guided sessions on a screen.

Are my child’s Coach Engine answers private?

Yes. The Coach Engine runs on your own device and does not send answers to us or to anyone else. At the end of a session you can export a summary if you want to keep it.

What devices does the Coach Engine work on?

It runs in the web browser on iPhone, iPad and desktop. There is no app to download.
