Privacy policy

What this website collects, and what it doesn’t.

Last updated 15 November 2026

Who we are

The Thinking Dojo is run by SIGNAL333 Ventures in the United Kingdom. You can reach us at hello@alpha333.org.

This policy covers this website and purchases made through it.

The short version

  • This website has no accounts and no sign-up forms.
  • This website sets no cookies.
  • There is no advertising and there are no advertising trackers.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children through this website.
  • Your child’s Coach Engine answers stay on your own device.

Counting visits

We count page views with GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service. It uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It records the page viewed, the site you came from, your browser type, your screen size and your approximate location, such as your country. We use this only to see which pages are read.

Fonts

The typefaces on this website load from Google Fonts. To send them, Google’s servers receive your device’s IP address, as any web server does.

When you buy

Checkout is run by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy takes your payment and handles tax and receipts. We never see your card details.

We receive your name, your email address and what you bought, so that we can deliver your purchase and help if something goes wrong. Lemon Squeezy’s own privacy policy applies to the checkout.

When you email us

If you email us, we use your address and your message only to reply to you.

Children

This website is written for parents, teachers and home educators. The free cards and the printed packs are used on paper and send nothing to us.

The Coach Engine

The Coach Engine is a file that runs in the web browser on your own device. It does not send your child’s answers to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them. It needs no internet connection once it is open.

Answers are held only for the session in progress. At the end of a session you can export a text summary to keep. If you do not, the answers are not kept.

The Coach Engine remembers which belts have been completed in the browser’s storage on that device. It also stores the date it was unlocked and a shortened reference to your access key. Clearing the browser’s data removes all of this.

How long we keep information

We keep order details for as long as we need them to support your purchase and to meet our legal duties. We keep emails for as long as we need them to deal with your question.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@alpha333.org.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
